Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what clients most often ask before starting a project with Kreative Solution.

What services does Kreative Solution offer?

Kreative Solution offers three core services: interior design for homes and apartments, residential and commercial architecture (including Vastu-compliant design and permit drawings), and end-to-end turnkey project execution across Goa.

How much does interior design or architecture cost in Goa?

Costs vary depending on the size of the space, scope of work and material choices. Kreative Solution offers a free initial consultation to understand your project and provide a tailored estimate — contact Padma Sawant directly for accurate pricing.

How long does a typical residential project take?

A typical apartment or house interior design project takes a few weeks for design and a few months for execution, depending on scope. Larger architecture and turnkey projects take longer; exact timelines are shared after the initial consultation.

Do you offer Vastu-compliant design?

Yes. Kreative Solution provides Vastu consultation and Vastu-compliant architectural and interior design for clients who want their homes and offices planned according to Vastu principles.

Do you work on projects outside Goa?

Kreative Solution is based in Goa, India, and primarily serves residential and commercial clients across Goa. Contact us to discuss projects located elsewhere.

How do I get started with Kreative Solution?

Reach out via phone, WhatsApp or email to book a free consultation. Padma Sawant will personally discuss your project, vision and budget before proposing a design plan.
